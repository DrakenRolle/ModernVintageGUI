# The five sample windows: opened by command and by the K hotkey, driven by name and
# photographed. The chat stays on so the ShowChatMessage lines of the samples are in the
# full window pictures.
#
# Run with:   ZIngameShots\ingame-screenshot.ps1 -Steps .\steps\samples.txt
#
# Names: the buttons and fields are the _Name arguments in Samples/*.cs, a tab page is
# "<Caption>_tab", and the bars of a split panel are "<split name>_splitter0", "_splitter1" and
# so on - browser_splitter0 and screen_splitter0 in the explorer, upper_splitter0 and
# screen_splitter0 in the dashboard.

wait 2s

cmd .mvsui sample                    # lists the samples in the chat
wait 5
